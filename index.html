About Novovine

Renewal & growth.

Novovine is an ingredient sourcing and distribution company. We supply botanical extracts, functional actives, creatine and vitamins to the contract manufacturers who make the industry's products, and to the founders starting new beverage and supplement brands.

We're a small team, and that's how we like to work: you talk to the people who handle your order, and your questions get a direct answer.
